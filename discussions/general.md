## 📋 Posting Guidelines

### ✅ Do's

- 🎭 **Be creative** - Share your unique setups and ideas
- 🤝 **Be helpful** - Answer questions when you can
- 🎨 **Share visuals** - Screenshots make everything better
- 🔍 **Search first** - Check if your question was already answered
- 🌟 **Be encouraging** - Celebrate others' setups

### ❌ Don'ts

- 🔥 **No flaming** - Keep it friendly and constructive
- 📢 **No spam** - Avoid repetitive or promotional content
- 🔧 **No tech support** - Use Q&A category for troubleshooting
- 💡 **No feature requests** - Use Ideas category for suggestions
